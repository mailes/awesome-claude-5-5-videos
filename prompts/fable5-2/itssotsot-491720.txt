# A toy-room adventure game
Author: @itssotsot
Original post: https://x.com/itssotsot/status/2119909017216491720
Prompt & live remake: https://workskill.tools/ai-videos/fable5-2/itssotsot-491720

I built a simple Toy Story-themed game with Fable 5.2, and here&#x27;s how!
At first, Fable refused to create anything from the Toy Story universe, so it just recreated the styles and it did a damn good job! Look at the lightning, the dust particles, the room itself. How can you not fall in love with something like that?
Here&#x27;s the prompt I used:
&quot;I want you to create a three.js third-person game where I am Woody from Toy Story inside the house, and I can walk and jump through furniture and objects and find the other characters. Make sure to capture the aesthetic and style of the movie.&quot;
