# Painting a portrait stroke by stroke
Author: @internetphysics
Original post: https://x.com/internetphysics/status/2119903808935313778
Prompt & live remake: https://workskill.tools/ai-videos/fable5-2/internetphysics-313778

Fable 5.2 actually paints Sam Altman
This is not painting with code or using scripts to convert image pixel data into digital paint. Instead, it works on a digital simulation of real oil paint, where new colors can only be created by properly mixing existing paints with the available brushes
Fable looks at the reference image and creates the painting by working in the browser and looking at screenshots of its own work as it progresses
