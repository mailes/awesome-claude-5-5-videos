# A sticker sign made of tiny prisms
Author: @Maaaaaaadiii
Original post: https://x.com/Maaaaaaadiii/status/2119885527243714946
Prompt & live remake: https://workskill.tools/ai-videos/fable5-2/maaaaaaadiii-714946

I asked Claude Fable 5.2 to choose its own tools and build something for me.
Canvas 2D drew the art, a shader turned every sticker into tiny prisms; Three.js hung the chains; Web Audio rang the bells. No images, no audio files.
