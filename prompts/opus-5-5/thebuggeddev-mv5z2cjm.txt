# Claude用Three.js将包装设计还原为3D纸盒
Author: @thebuggeddev
Original post: https://x.com/thebuggeddev/status/2109524968686100500
Prompt & live remake: https://workskill.tools/ai-videos/opus-5-5/thebuggeddev-mv5z2cjm

Gave Claude a photo of packaging concept and asked it to recreate it in 3D.

It built that entire carton using math, including curved panels, corrugated edges, transparent windows, and a folding lid.

Made with Three.js and WebGPU by Claude Opus 5.5 https://t.co/2JxEiDWFSK
